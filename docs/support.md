# Support

Jewelers Handbook is an offline reference handbook for jewelers for iPhone and iPad. All content is built into the app and available without an internet connection.

**Contact the developer:** [dreamcomenull@gmail.com](mailto:dreamcomenull@gmail.com)
I reply to every message as soon as I can.

### FAQ

**How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?**
Email [dreamcomenull@gmail.com](mailto:dreamcomenull@gmail.com). Include your device model, iOS version, app version (shown in the App Store), and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. The handbook works fully offline. You only need internet to install and update the app from the App Store.

**Do I need an account?**
No. The app has no accounts or sign-in.

**What data does the app collect?**
Only anonymous crash reports through Apple's built-in mechanism, if you have turned on "Share With App Developers" in iOS settings. No ads, analytics, or tracking. Details: [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy' | relative_url }}).
