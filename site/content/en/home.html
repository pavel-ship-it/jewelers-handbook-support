What is Jewelers Handbook?

Jewelers Handbook is an offline reference and calculator kit made by a working chainmaker for jewelers, students and hobbyists. It answers the everyday bench questions: how many rings and how much wire a Byzantine chain needs, how heavy a sheet or ring blank will be, which setting suits a fragile stone, what ring size a customer’s 17 mm means in US or UK sizes.

Every number comes from a named source, such as GIA articles, supplier catalogs and standards, and the app lists all of them. Where no reliable source exists, the field is left empty instead of guessed. Calculations of weight, carats and sizes are clearly marked as estimates.

How it works

  1. Find it. Open a section from the home screen or type in Search the handbook. Search understands both English and Russian names.
  2. Enter your sizes. Choose the metal and enter wire, ring or stone dimensions. Results update as you type, with warnings when a chain won’t assemble.
  3. Check it at 1:1. Tap Actual size to see the model on screen at its real size, then save the calculation to My pieces.

Features

  • ⛓

    Chain calculators

    Byzantine, anchor (cable) and Moscow bit: number of rings or links, wire length and chain weight from your sizes, with a check of whether the weave will assemble. Correct the result with a test piece.

    How the calculators work
  • ⚖

    Approximate chain weight

    Bismarck, rope, curb, Figaro 3/1 and box chains: weight per centimeter from supplier tables, clearly marked as approximate.

    About approximate chains
  • ◈

    3D at actual size

    Rotate and zoom chains, cuts, settings, clasps and rings in 3D. Actual size shows them 1:1, scaled automatically on known iPhones and iPads.

    Actual size and calibration
  • 💎

    32 gemstones

    Group, Mohs hardness, refractive index, density, colors, toughness (GIA 1987), common treatments, ultrasonic cleaning and care, each with sources.

    Stone cards
  • ◇

    15 cuts

    From round brilliant and princess to rose and cabochon: shape, facets, typical proportions and fragile spots, each with a 3D model.

    Cut cards
  • 💍

    Settings and finder

    13 setting types, from prongs and bezel to pavé and tension. The finder takes a stone and a cut and shows which settings are recommended and which are not.

    Setting finder
  • 🔒

    14 clasp types

    Lobster, spring ring, box, toggle and more, with 3D diagrams that open and close, pros and cons, and a clasp finder by the features you need.

    Clasps
  • 📏

    Bench tools

    Size → carat estimates stone weight from dimensions, Ring size converts EU, RU, US and UK, and Metal weight covers wire, sheet and ring blanks.

    Tools
  • ★

    My pieces and favorites

    Save any calculation with its 3D thumbnail, duplicate it or export to a file. Star the entries you use most.

Private and offline by design

  • Works fully offline. All catalogs, calculators and 3D models are built into the app.
  • No account, sign-up or sign-in. No ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your pieces, favorites and settings stay on your device.

Read the Privacy Policy.

Good to know

Languages. The app is available in English and Russian and follows your device language.

Calculations are estimates. Weight, carat and size results depend on the alloy, tolerances and finishing. Always check against a test piece, scales and a gauge before making a piece.
